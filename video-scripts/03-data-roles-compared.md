# Video 3 — Data engineer vs AI engineer vs data scientist vs data analyst

**Length:** 90s · **Placement:** reused on every Stackle masterclass page, YouTube, social
**Course-neutral:** no course name, price, weeks, tools or job-posting stats.

---

**0:00–0:07 · HOOK**
VO (Chris, to camera): "Data engineer, AI engineer, data scientist, data analyst. All four job titles say 'data'. Each one does a very different job."
ON SCREEN: 4 roles. 1 company. Who does what?

**0:07–0:14 · INTRO**
VO: "I'm Chris. I've run more than 200 senior technical interviews from the hiring side, and I've worked with all four of these roles. Let's take one company, a food delivery app."
ON SCREEN: Example: a food delivery app

**0:14–0:28 · DATA ENGINEER**
VO: "The data engineer gets every order, every rider location and every payment into one place the company can trust, every hour, without errors. What they build is pipelines and clean tables."
ON SCREEN: Data engineer · Makes the data reliable · Builds: pipelines, tables

**0:28–0:40 · DATA ANALYST**
VO: "The data analyst asks why orders dropped on Tuesday in one city, and finds the answer. What they produce is answers, reports and dashboards."
ON SCREEN: Data analyst · Explains what happened · Builds: answers, dashboards

**0:40–0:52 · DATA SCIENTIST**
VO: "The data scientist predicts how many riders the app needs at seven on Friday, and tests whether a new discount really works. What they produce is forecasts, experiments and models."
ON SCREEN: Data scientist · Predicts and tests · Builds: forecasts, experiments, models

**0:52–1:04 · AI ENGINEER**
VO: "The AI engineer builds the assistant that answers 'where's my order?' by connecting a language model to the company's data, then measuring how often it gets things right. What they build is AI features."
ON SCREEN: AI engineer · Ships AI into the product · Builds: AI features, evaluations

**1:04–1:18 · WHAT AI CHANGES FOR ALL FOUR**
VO: "AI now writes the first draft of the code in all four roles. What sets each role apart is the judgment. The engineer asks whether the data is correct. The analyst asks whether it's the right question. The scientist asks whether the effect is real. The AI engineer asks whether the feature behaves safely."
ON SCREEN: AI writes the first draft. You learn the judgment that gets you hired.

**1:18–1:30 · WHICH ONE FITS YOU / END CARD**
VO: "If you like building systems, look at data engineering. If you like asking why, look at analysis. If you like probability and experiments, data science. If you like shipping product, AI engineering. Not sure which one is you? Book a call at stackle.io."
ON SCREEN: Systems → DE · "Why?" → Analyst · Experiments → DS · Product → AI eng · **stackle.io** · [Book a call]
