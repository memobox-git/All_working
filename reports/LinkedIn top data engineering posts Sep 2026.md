# Top data engineering LinkedIn posts, September 2026 (200+ reactions)

We found **59 LinkedIn posts from September 2026 with at least 200 reactions** that are about data engineering interviews (13 posts) or data engineering tools (46 posts). Every count below was read from the post's own public page on 19 October 2026, and every date comes from the post's ID, so both are exact. The biggest tool post was Liam Wynne's launch of ERD Studio, a VS Code extension for data modeling (**2,550 reactions, 202 comments**). The biggest interview post was a "design a pipeline for 1 TB a day" challenge from Tech Interview Titans (**961 reactions**). One gap matters: the method reaches posts from **17–30 September** only. Posts from 1–16 September could not be found, so the list is complete for the second half of the month, not the first.

## What the top posts have in common

- **A launch or a clear explainer beats a hot take.** The two biggest posts were a free tool built by a practitioner (ERD Studio) and a plain explainer of the medallion architecture. Both are things people save.
- **Lists and cheat sheets travel.** "50 PySpark commands", "100 SQL questions", "20 Databricks resources" and "Top 50 Spark performance issues" all passed 400 reactions.
- **"X vs Y" works for tools.** Fabric vs Databricks passed 400 reactions twice in one week (Yash Jadiya 599, Monita B 411), with nearly the same text.
- **Interview posts win with one concrete question.** The format is "asked at Accenture", "count the rows without running the query" or "design for 1 TB a day", not general advice.

## How we found and checked these posts

Web search could not find recent LinkedIn posts. Its index held almost nothing newer than June 2026, and LinkedIn blocks profile pages for logged-out visitors. Each public post page does carry its exact reaction and comment counts, plus a list of about ten related recent posts. We started from a few September posts and followed those related lists across about 1,300 post pages. That reached about 5,500 posts, of which about 2,700 were from September. LinkedIn's related list only covers roughly the last 30 days, which is why 1–16 September is missing. The date of each post comes from its LinkedIn activity ID, whose top bits are the publish time in milliseconds.

From the 80 September posts with 200+ reactions, we removed posts that were not about data engineering interviews or tools. Those were data analyst roadmaps, Excel, Power BI interview sets, and general Python. Three posts that sit right on the line were also left out, all general SQL: "EXISTS vs IN" by Rhythm (589), "Pandas vs SQL" by Carol Mello (490), and "ROW_NUMBER vs RANK vs DENSE_RANK" by Abhishek Gupta (206). LinkedIn's "reactions" count every reaction type, not only likes. The full list, with activity IDs, is in `research_notes/LinkedIn top data engineering posts Sep 2026/posts.csv`.
