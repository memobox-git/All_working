# SleekBuys should start by answering one question: "What will I actually pay for this phone, and should I buy now?"

The [audit](SleekBuys%20monetization%20and%20marketing%20audit.md) found a site that tries to do everything and solves nothing in particular. It mixes:
- phone news, beauty reviews and fashion guides;
- a coupons page with 2019 demo coupons;
- a travel page with no travel on it.

This plan starts from the other end: pick one problem that Indian shoppers really have, solve it better than anyone, and let the money follow from the solution.

**The problem: buying a phone in a sale is confusing.** A sale page doesn't show the price you will actually pay:
- **Bank offers carry fine print.** Flipkart's 2026 Big Billion Days headline offered Axis Bank cardholders "10% Instant Discount up to INR 17,250". The fine print capped the discount on phones at **₹1,000–1,750**. The cap changed with the phone's group, EMI vs full payment, and the day of the sale, and the Flipkart Axis Bank card was excluded ([Axis Bank terms](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/terms-and-conditions-for-flipkart-big-billion-day-sale-offer-on-axis-bank-cards.pdf)).
- **Card offers aren't always instant.** Some are cashback paid later.
- **"No-cost" EMI isn't always free.** It can hide processing fees and GST ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/no-cost-emi-popular-available-but-misunderstood-expert-busts-myths-hidden-charges-risks-505406-2025-12-06)).
- **Exchange value depends on where you live.** It varies by pincode ([Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/flipkart-bank-offers-october-2026-axis-sbi-icici-cards-checked)).
- **Not every "sale price" is a deal.** About **one product in five** cost more in the weeks before a big sale than in the month before ([Price History audit of 325 products](https://pricehistoryapp.com/sale-events)).

**Why this is a big market.** Phones are the biggest thing people buy in these sales: **38–40%** of the ₹24,000 crore spent in the first two days of the 2026 festive sales ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/10/ecomm-firms-goods-worth-rs-24k-cr-first-two-days-festive-sales-redseer)).

**What exists today.**
- Price-history tools (BuyHatke, Price History) tell you whether a price is low.
- Deal sites write one-off articles about bank offers.
- "Best phones under ₹20,000" lists are everywhere, and Google's AI now answers that kind of query itself.
- I found nobody who, phone by phone and day by day, gives the real price for each card, says whether that price is a real low, and gives a clear **buy now, wait or skip** verdict. My search was limited, so treat that gap as likely, not proven.

**Why SleekBuys can win it.** It already has:
- 449 product pages;
- a phone-focused YouTube channel with 7,380 subscribers;
- clear proof of what its audience wants. Its "X vs Y: which should you buy?" Shorts get about **twice the views** of its other Shorts (about 1,280 against 620), while its one generic "Top 7 deals" Short got 66.

**How the money follows.** Every part of the answer leads to a way to earn:
- "Buy now" → a tagged store link.
- "The best price needs a card you don't have" → a credit-card page, paying ₹441–1,400 per approved card.
- "Wait" → a WhatsApp or Telegram alert, which builds the audience SleekBuys doesn't yet own.

**Timing.**
1. Test it by hand on 10 phones before Diwali (8 November).
2. Grow to 30 phones in November and December.
3. Launch it fully for the Republic Day sales in mid-January.

## The promise, the customer and the product

**One-line promise:** "Before you buy a phone, SleekBuys tells you the real price you'll pay with your card today, whether it's a genuine low, and whether to buy now or wait."

**The customer:** someone in India buying a phone on Amazon or Flipkart during a sale, with a mid-range budget of roughly ₹10,000–40,000. (The budget range is a choice of focus, not a research finding.)

**What they get for each phone: a "Real Price" card.** The example below uses real figures from a Flipkart iPhone 17 listing on 19 October 2026 ([Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/flipkart-bank-offers-october-2026-axis-sbi-icici-cards-checked)). The last two rows show what SleekBuys would add.

| iPhone 17 (256 GB) on Flipkart, checked 19 Oct 2026 | |
|---|---|
| MRP | ₹99,900 |
| Listed price | ₹86,999 |
| With a Flipkart Axis or Flipkart SBI card | ₹82,999 (₹4,000 cashback, paid later; date not published) |
| No-cost EMI, 6 months | ₹84,499 in total, ₹1,500 more than the card price |
| Exchange | Up to ₹59,050, but not offered at every pincode |
| **SleekBuys adds: is it a real low?** | Price compared with the lowest of the last 90 days |
| **SleekBuys adds: verdict** | **Buy now / Wait / Skip**, the reason in one line, and a better phone for the same real price if there is one |

**Where it lives:**
1. **One page per phone** on the site, updated at the same URL, with "checked at [time, date]" on every price.
2. **A daily sale hub** listing the 10–30 phones people search for most.
3. **A WhatsApp Channel and a Telegram channel.** A request like "tell me when this phone's real price drops below ₹X" is the reason to join.
4. **A daily YouTube Short and Instagram Reel**: "Real price of [phone] today: buy or wait?", linked to the phone's page.

**Rules that protect trust.** Trust is the product, so these are not optional:
- **Independent verdicts.** No brand can pay for a verdict. Sponsored content, if it ever comes, is separate and labelled #ad.
- **Disclosed links.** Affiliate links carry a clear disclosure line.
- **Amazon pricing rules.** Amazon only allows prices pulled from its own data feed, refreshed at least every 24 hours ([Amazon operating agreement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement)). Access to that feed needs 10 sales in 30 days. Until then, show the **saving from each offer** (for example "Axis card: saves ₹1,250 on phones above ₹12,500") and a "check today's price" button, not a typed-in Amazon price.

## Solve it by hand first, then automate

Don't build software yet. Prove people want the answer by doing it manually for a few phones.

| Phase | Dates | What happens | Who does it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Remove the blockers** | 24–26 Oct | Only what would undermine the launch: delete the 110 pirated-Windows posts, tag every store link (Cuelinks or EarnKaro, plus the Amazon tag), add the affiliate disclosure and privacy pages, connect Google Analytics and Search Console, fix "$" to "₹". (Details are in the audit.) | Site admin |
| **1. Test on 10 phones** | 27 Oct – 8 Nov (Diwali) | Choose the 10 phones from the YouTube comments, the 449 product pages and the sale best-seller lists. Check prices and offer terms twice a day in a spreadsheet. Publish 10 Real Price pages and a Diwali hub. Start the WhatsApp and Telegram channels. Post one "Real price today" Short or Reel a day. | 1 researcher for 2–3 hours a day; the existing video editor |
| **2. Learn and widen to 30 phones** | 9 Nov – 31 Dec | Keep the phones and formats that worked. Add the 90-day price line by logging prices daily. Run Black Friday (27 Nov) and year-end sale hubs. Publish one long video a week: "Real prices this week: the best phones under ₹20K / ₹30K". Publish one honest card page: "Which card gets the lowest real price this sale". | Same team, plus a part-time writer |
| **3. Full launch** | Republic Day sales, mid-Jan 2027 | 30–50 phones. Prepare the hub 3 weeks early with sale dates and bank-offer terms. Put ₹500–1,000 a day of Meta ads behind the best Reels to grow WhatsApp and Telegram (India's median ad cost is about $1.35 per 1,000 impressions, per [Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/india)). Apply for YouTube Shopping tags. | Team plus ad budget |
| **4. Automate and extend** | Feb–Mar 2027 | Only once it's working: switch to Amazon's data feed, which needs 10 sales in 30 days ([Amazon](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/introduction)); automate price alerts; add a second category (earbuds or laptops). | Developer |

**What to stop doing while this runs:**
- daily phone-news rewrites;
- beauty and fashion product posts;
- the travel and coupon pages;
- generic "Top 7 deals" lists;
- launch-hype Shorts with no verdict.

Each one takes time away from the one thing that should make SleekBuys known.

## How the money follows the solution

| What the shopper is told | What SleekBuys earns | Rate (from the audit) |
|---|---|---|
| "Buy now. Real price on Flipkart with an Axis card is ₹X, the lowest in 90 days." | A store commission through a tagged link | Amazon pays 0.32–0.63% on phones. Through Cuelinks, Flipkart pays up to 7.2% (Flipkart's own page lists up to 5% on mobiles). |
| "Buy it with matching earbuds or a case." | A higher-rate accessory commission | Amazon pays 2.2–2.5%; boAt up to 7.2% |
| "The lowest real price needs a card you don't have." | A credit-card referral, **only when the card genuinely fits** | ₹441–1,400 per approved card ([vCommission](https://www.vcommission.com/blog/publishers/festive-cpa-opportunities-high-paying-credit-card-campaigns-for-affiliates/)) |
| "Wait. It was cheaper last month. We'll alert you when it drops." | An owned subscriber, who earns at the next sale | Free reach every sale, with no dependence on Google |
| "Real price" videos | YouTube Shopping tags. YouTube says these get more than twice the clicks of description links. | Retailer commission ([FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/493334/youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-india-expansion.html)) |

A "wait" verdict costs a sale today, but it is what makes the "buy now" verdicts believable.

## How to know it's working: three checkpoints

These starting bars come from SleekBuys' own numbers where they exist. Reset them once Analytics gives a real baseline.

| Checkpoint | Keep going if at least two of these are true | If not |
|---|---|---|
| **After Diwali (9 Nov)** | "Real price" Shorts beat the channel's comparison average of about **1,280 views** · **300+** people join WhatsApp or Telegram · readers ask for **20+** specific phones in comments or messages | Change the format (more comparison, less price), not the problem |
| **Year end (31 Dec)** | Real Price pages earn more per visitor than other pages · **1,000+** WhatsApp, Telegram and email subscribers · at least one card referral is approved | Narrow to a single price band (for example under ₹20K) |
| **After Republic Day (end Jan)** | Store clicks and earnings in the sale beat Diwali · subscribers keep growing between sales | Rethink the problem before automating |

**Track weekly:** views per "Real price" Short, page visits to store clicks, alert sign-ups, phones requested by readers, and affiliate earnings by page.

## What's needed from the client

- **One person** who checks offers and prices twice a day during sales; this is the core of the product.
- **The video editor** already making Shorts, switched to the "Real price today" format.
- **Admin access** to fix the blockers in phase 0.
- **A small ad budget** from January (₹500–1,000 a day to start).
- **A decision** that SleekBuys is a phone-buying helper first, and that everything else waits.

## Caveats

- **The gap may not be fully open.** I didn't find a site that gives per-phone, per-card real prices with a buy-or-wait verdict, but my search was limited, and Google's AI Mode is moving into shopping fast. Speed and an owned audience (WhatsApp, Telegram, email) protect against both.
- **Pre-sale price rises.** The "one in five" figure comes from a price-tracking company's own audit of 325 products across all categories, not just phones.
- **Example offer terms.** The bank-offer example uses Axis Bank's 2026 Big Billion Days terms. Other banks and sales use different caps, which is exactly the confusion SleekBuys would clear up.
- **Commission rates.** The rates come from the audit; check them in your own Amazon and network dashboards.
