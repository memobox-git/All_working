# SleekBuys should start by answering one question: "What will I actually pay for this phone, and should I buy now?"

The [audit](SleekBuys%20monetization%20and%20marketing%20audit.md) found a site that tries to do everything and solves nothing in particular. It mixes:
- phone news, beauty reviews and fashion guides;
- a coupons page with 2019 demo coupons;
- a travel page with no travel on it.

This plan starts from the other end: pick one problem that Indian shoppers really have, solve it better than anyone, and let the money follow from the solution.

**The problem: buying a phone in a sale is confusing.** A sale page doesn't show the price you will actually pay:
- **Bank offers carry fine print.** Flipkart's 2026 Big Billion Days headline offered Axis Bank cardholders "10% Instant Discount up to INR 17,250". The fine print capped the discount on phones at **₹1,000–1,750**. The cap changed with the phone's group, EMI vs full payment, and the day of the sale, and the Flipkart Axis Bank card was excluded ([Axis Bank terms](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/terms-and-conditions-for-flipkart-big-billion-day-sale-offer-on-axis-bank-cards.pdf)).
- **Card offers aren't always instant.** Some are cashback paid later.
- **"No-cost" EMI isn't always free.** It can hide processing fees and GST ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/no-cost-emi-popular-available-but-misunderstood-expert-busts-myths-hidden-charges-risks-505406-2025-12-06)).
- **Exchange value depends on where you live.** It varies by pincode ([Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/flipkart-bank-offers-october-2026-axis-sbi-icici-cards-checked)).
- **Not every "sale price" is a deal.** About **one product in five** cost more in the weeks before a big sale than in the month before ([Price History audit of 325 products](https://pricehistoryapp.com/sale-events)).

**Why this is a big market.** Phones are the biggest thing people buy in these sales: **38–40%** of the ₹24,000 crore spent in the first two days of the 2026 festive sales ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/10/ecomm-firms-goods-worth-rs-24k-cr-first-two-days-festive-sales-redseer)).

**What exists today.**
- Price-history tools (BuyHatke, Price History) tell you whether a price is low.
- Deal sites write one-off articles about bank offers.
- "Best phones under ₹20,000" lists are everywhere, and Google's AI now answers that kind of query itself.
- **Correction to an earlier draft:** this space is not empty. BuyHatke's price-checking tool (its Chrome extension has about 1 million users) already promises to "Spot price hikes before sales", "See the real effective price" and "Get quick verdict: Buy / Skip" ([BuyHatke](https://buyhatke.com/)). What's still open is narrower: a **phone-buying verdict delivered as short videos and WhatsApp posts** that says which phone to buy, with which card, and whether to wait. See "How competitors treat this problem" below.

**Why SleekBuys can win it.** It already has:
- 449 product pages;
- a phone-focused YouTube channel with 7,380 subscribers;
- clear proof of what its audience wants. Its "X vs Y: which should you buy?" Shorts get about **twice the views** of its other Shorts (about 1,280 against 620), while its one generic "Top 7 deals" Short got 66.

**How the money follows.** Every part of the answer leads to a way to earn:
- "Buy now" → a tagged store link.
- "The best price needs a card you don't have" → a credit-card page, paying ₹441–1,400 per approved card.
- "Wait" → a WhatsApp or Telegram alert, which builds the audience SleekBuys doesn't yet own.

**Timing.**
1. Test it by hand on 10 phones before Diwali (8 November).
2. Grow to 30 phones in November and December.
3. Launch it fully for the Republic Day sales in mid-January.

**What's left open for SleekBuys:**
1. **The phone choice and the price in one answer.** Tools check one product at a time, and deal posts list one deal at a time. Neither says "for ₹20K with an SBI card, buy the Vivo on Amazon today rather than the Redmi". That combines SleekBuys' proven "X vs Y" format with the real price.
2. **Your card, not "up to".** Only a tiny Firefox add-on works out the price for a specific card.
3. **Video and WhatsApp, not installs.** A 30-second Short or a WhatsApp post reaches people who will never install an extension.

**What SleekBuys should not do:** build its own price tracker or alerts app. BuyHatke and Price History have years of price data and millions of installs, and Amazon now shows its own price history for free. Use their public price charts to check "is this a real low?", and spend SleekBuys' effort on the verdict.

## Is the problem worth it? 6 out of 10, and only in the narrower form

| Factor | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How much it hurts | **8** | Fine print cuts "up to ₹17,250" to ₹1,000–1,750 on phones. Viral "effective prices" need fact-checks. 1 in 5 products cost more just before sales. |
| How big it is | **8** | Phones were 38–40% of the ₹24,000 crore spent in the first two days of the 2026 festive sales, and big sales come round several times a year |
| How open the space is | **3** | BuyHatke already promises the effective price and verdict; Price History, deal communities, Google and super.money are all moving in |
| SleekBuys' ability to win | **5** | It has a phone YouTube channel (comparisons get twice the views) and 449 phone and product pages. It has no price data, no app, a damaged site and no owned audience. |
| Money attached | **6** | Phone commissions are thin (0.32–0.63% on Amazon), but card referrals (₹441–1,400) and accessories (2–7%) add up |
| **Overall** | **6 / 10** | Worth testing cheaply as a content brand, not worth building as a tool |

It scores higher (about 7) if the Diwali test shows people want the combined "which phone + which card + buy or wait" verdict on video and WhatsApp. It scores lower (about 4) if SleekBuys tries to out-build BuyHatke on price tracking.

**The checkpoint after Diwali is the real test.** If Real Price Shorts don't beat the channel's comparison average (about 1,280 views) and fewer than 300 people join WhatsApp or Telegram, drop the price angle and go back to plain "which phone should you buy" comparisons, which already work for the channel.

## The promise, the customer and the product

**One-line promise:** "Before you buy a phone, SleekBuys tells you the real price you'll pay with your card today, whether it's a genuine low, and whether to buy now or wait."

**The customer:** someone in India buying a phone on Amazon or Flipkart during a sale, with a mid-range budget of roughly ₹10,000–40,000. (The budget range is a choice of focus, not a research finding.)

**What they get for each phone: a "Real Price" card.** The example below uses real figures from a Flipkart iPhone 17 listing on 19 October 2026 ([Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/flipkart-bank-offers-october-2026-axis-sbi-icici-cards-checked)). The last two rows show what SleekBuys would add.

| iPhone 17 (256 GB) on Flipkart, checked 19 Oct 2026 | |
|---|---|
| MRP | ₹99,900 |
| Listed price | ₹86,999 |
| With a Flipkart Axis or Flipkart SBI card | ₹82,999 (₹4,000 cashback, paid later; date not published) |
| No-cost EMI, 6 months | ₹84,499 in total, ₹1,500 more than the card price |
| Exchange | Up to ₹59,050, but not offered at every pincode |
| **SleekBuys adds: is it a real low?** | Price compared with the lowest of the last 90 days |
| **SleekBuys adds: verdict** | **Buy now / Wait / Skip**, the reason in one line, and a better phone for the same real price if there is one |

**Where it lives:**
1. **One page per phone** on the site, updated at the same URL, with "checked at [time, date]" on every price.
2. **A daily sale hub** listing the 10–30 phones people search for most.
3. **A WhatsApp Channel and a Telegram channel.** A request like "tell me when this phone's real price drops below ₹X" is the reason to join.
4. **A daily YouTube Short and Instagram Reel**: "Real price of [phone] today: buy or wait?", linked to the phone's page.

**Rules that protect trust.** Trust is the product, so these are not optional:
- **Independent verdicts.** No brand can pay for a verdict. Sponsored content, if it ever comes, is separate and labelled #ad.
- **Disclosed links.** Affiliate links carry a clear disclosure line.
- **Amazon pricing rules.** Amazon only allows prices pulled from its own data feed, refreshed at least every 24 hours ([Amazon operating agreement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement)). Access to that feed needs 10 sales in 30 days. Until then, show the **saving from each offer** (for example "Axis card: saves ₹1,250 on phones above ₹12,500") and a "check today's price" button, not a typed-in Amazon price.

### How to run it day to day

The team uses the [Real Price Checker](https://claude.ai/artifact/T9EzhaxPRf4JMo7243Rcto). You type in one phone's prices and bank offers, and it gives the real price for each card, a buy-now-or-wait verdict, and ready-to-copy posts for WhatsApp and Telegram, a 30-second Short, and the website.

| Time | What happens | About how long |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Check the 10 phones on Amazon and Flipkart: listed price, the 90-day low (Amazon's "Price history" link; a price-history chart for Flipkart), each bank offer's %, maximum, minimum order and full-payment-or-EMI, and any EMI fee. Enter them in the checker. | 5 minutes a phone, so about 50 minutes |
| 10:00 | Post the morning real prices to WhatsApp and Telegram from the checker's text. Update each phone's page on the site with the website note (savings, not Amazon prices). | 20 minutes |
| 11:00–15:00 | Film one Short or Reel on the most-asked phone, using the checker's script and real footage of the phone or the store page. | 2–3 hours, editor |
| 19:00 | Re-check the phones whose price or offers move most, and post changes only. | 30 minutes |
| 21:00 | Read the comments and messages. Every "which phone for ₹X with my card?" question becomes a candidate for tomorrow's list or Short. | 15 minutes |

**What to stop doing while this runs:**
- daily phone-news rewrites;
- beauty and fashion product posts;
- the travel and coupon pages;
- generic "Top 7 deals" lists;
- launch-hype Shorts with no verdict.

Each one takes time away from the one thing that should make SleekBuys known.

## Should SleekBuys add an AI bot? Later, and only on top of checked data

**An AI chat on its own won't set SleekBuys apart, because every big player already has one:**
- **Amazon.** Its assistant (Rufus, now "Alexa for Shopping") has been used by more than 1 crore Indian customers ([Amazon India](https://aboutamazon.in/news/retail/rufus-ai-shopping-assistant-launch-in-india)), and it answers "What's the price history?" in India ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-to-check-amazon-price-history)).
- **Flipkart.** It has run its Flippi assistant since 2023 and is reported to be building a standalone successor ([Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/technology-news/retail-tech/flipkart-roll-standalone-conversational-ai-shopping-assistant/)).
- **Google, super.money and Perplexity.** Google answers shopping questions in Gemini and AI Mode. super.money's agents buy on Flipkart when a target price is hit. Airtel gave Perplexity Pro free to its subscribers ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/indias-ai-revolution-airtel-hand-delivers-perplexity-pro-to-370-mn-challenging-googles-reign-75571.htm)).
- **BuyHatke.** It already markets "AI Insights" and has 1 crore+ Android downloads ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.buyhatke.assistant)), so it could add chat quickly.

**A bot can stand out only if it does three things those can't:**
1. **Stays neutral across stores.** Amazon's assistant won't tell you Flipkart is cheaper today.
2. **Uses your card and today's checked offers**, with the cap, minimum order and EMI fine print.
3. **Lives on WhatsApp.** A shopper sends "Vivo V80, SBI card" and gets the real price, the verdict and an alert offer back.

**What could go wrong:**

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| **The bot invents prices or offers.** One wrong "real price" ruins trust, and misleading claims are a consumer-law risk. | The bot answers **only from the team's checked data** for that day. For any phone it hasn't got, it says "not checked today" and explains how to check. AI understands the question and words the reply; the numbers always come from the sheet. |
| **WhatsApp's rules.** Since 15 Jan 2026, WhatsApp's business platform bans general-purpose AI chatbots. Business bots for sales and support are still allowed ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform)). | Keep it a narrow "SleekBuys deals helper", not an ask-me-anything AI. |
| **Message costs.** Broadcast marketing messages cost about ₹0.86 each in India. Replies to customers are free or cheap; one report says a ₹0.115 fee per reply starts in Oct 2026, with 1,000 free a month ([TelecomTalk](https://telecomtalk.info/whatsappbusiness-price-reveal-telegram-arattai-may-benefit/1012403/); check Meta's rate card). | Broadcast through the free WhatsApp Channel. Use the bot only for one-to-one questions. |
| **Amazon's pricing rules** | Same as the website: show savings, or prices with a timestamp, and switch to Amazon's data feed once eligible. |

**When to build it:**
1. **Until Diwali:** no AI. Answer WhatsApp questions by hand and save every question. Those questions become the bot's brief.
2. **Nov–Dec, only if the Diwali checkpoint passes:** a simple WhatsApp bot that looks phones up in the Real Price data. Repoint the Botpress chatbot already on the site at the same data, or remove it, since today it only slows the pages down.
3. **For the Republic Day sales:** add "which phone under ₹X with my card?", choosing from the phones the team has checked.

**Score:** an AI bot on its own scores about **3/10**, because everyone has one. A WhatsApp bot built on SleekBuys' own daily checked card-price data, launched after demand is proven, scores about **7/10**.

## Where the data comes from

No single source gives Amazon and Flipkart prices, price history and bank offers together, so SleekBuys builds it in layers. **Don't scrape Amazon or Flipkart.**
- **Amazon.** Its Conditions of Use exclude "data mining, robots, or similar data gathering and extraction tools" and the collection of prices (as reproduced by [ConductAtlas](https://conductatlas.com/platform/amazon/amazon-conditions-of-use/provision/CA-P-018441/prohibition-on-data-mining-and-scraping-tools/)).
- **Your Amazon affiliate account.** The Associates agreement bars using Amazon's product data "for the purpose of aggregating, analyzing, extracting, or repurposing" it without written approval. It also limits storing prices to 24 hours (§11.3.3, items 8 and 13, [Amazon Associates India](https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement)).
- **The risk.** Breaking these rules risks the affiliate account, which is SleekBuys' main income.

| Data | Now (by hand, allowed) | Later (automated, allowed) |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon price today** | The product page | Amazon's Creators API, once SleekBuys makes 10 sales in 30 days. Show a date/time stamp and "accurate as of…" next to prices, and refresh at least every 24 hours (§11.3.3, item 14). |
| **Amazon price history** | Amazon's own **Price history** link next to the price: 30, 90 and 365 days ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-to-check-amazon-price-history)) | The **Keepa API**, a paid service with its own Amazon price history ([docs](https://keepa.com/api-docs/)). Its extension lists Amazon.in; confirm India API coverage and display rights with Keepa first. A third-party guide puts the entry plan at €49 a month. Don't build history from Amazon's own API without Amazon's written OK. |
| **Flipkart price today** | The product page | The **Flipkart Affiliate API**: product feed, change ("delta") feed, search, offers and deals of the day, up to 20 calls a second ([docs](https://affiliate.flipkart.com/api-docs/affiliate_index.html)). It needs a registered Flipkart affiliate account, and new sign-ups have been reported closed since 2018. Its terms bar use "in a similar or competitive platform" ([terms](https://affiliate.flipkart.com/api-docs/af_tou.html)), so ask Flipkart in writing whether a comparison site counts. |
| **Flipkart price history** | Read it off a public chart (BuyHatke, Price History) for the verdict; don't copy their data in bulk | SleekBuys' **own log**. Every price the team checks, or the API returns, goes in as a dated row. That builds a Flipkart history from day one. |
| **Bank offers** | The offer strip on the product page, plus the bank's own terms PDF | **No API exists anywhere.** Save each bank's terms PDF for each sale and have AI pull out the rules (%, cap, minimum, EMI or full payment) into the sheet. For example, Axis Bank's Big Billion Days caps came straight out of its PDF. A person then confirms them on the product page. This checked layer is the data nobody else has. |

**Start with one sheet.** Each check becomes one row:

| Date and time | Phone and variant | Store | Listed price | 90-day low | Card | % | Max | Min order | Full / EMI / both | EMI fee | Checked by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

From the first day this log is SleekBuys' own price and offer history. Later it can feed the Real Price Checker, the website and a WhatsApp bot. Move it to a proper database only when the sheet gets slow.

**One more Amazon rule:** if SleekBuys ever ships a mobile app, price tracking or price alerts in it need Amazon's written approval first (§21, item 4).

## How to know it's working: three checkpoints

These starting bars come from SleekBuys' own numbers where they exist. Reset them once Analytics gives a real baseline.

## Caveats

- **The gap is narrower than first thought.** BuyHatke already promises an effective price and a Buy/Skip verdict, and Google and Flipkart's super.money are moving in. SleekBuys' opening is the combined phone-and-card verdict as content. Speed and an owned audience (WhatsApp, Telegram, email) are its only protection.
- **Competitor checks were limited.** I couldn't confirm whether BuyHatke's effective price uses the shopper's own card. Smartprix and 91mobiles blocked direct page checks, so I couldn't see whether their phone pages show prices after bank offers.
- **Pre-sale price rises.** The "one in five" figure comes from a price-tracking company's own audit of 325 products across all categories, not just phones.
- **Example offer terms.** The bank-offer example uses Axis Bank's 2026 Big Billion Days terms. Other banks and sales use different caps, which is exactly the confusion SleekBuys would clear up.
- **Commission rates.** The rates come from the audit; check them in your own Amazon and network dashboards.
