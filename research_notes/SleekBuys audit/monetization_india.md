## Affiliate programs

**Amazon.in**
- **Rates by category** (3P table, Sep 2025; [Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/how-affiliates-can-earn-big-in-amazon-great-indian-festival-sale-2025/)):
  - Mobiles 0.32% (listed models) to 0.63% (others).
  - Headsets and mobile accessories 2.54%.
  - PCs, smartwatches, TVs and other electronics 2.22%.
  - Apparel, shoes, watches and home 3.17–6.34%.
  - Furniture and kitchen 3.80–5.07%.
  - Beauty 7.60%.
- **Tracking.** The cookie lasts 24 hours, plus 89 days for items added to the cart.
- **Disclosure.** "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" is required.
- **Price data.** Product data must come only through Amazon's API and can be cached for up to 24 hours. Prices need a timestamp if they aren't refreshed hourly. Extensions and price-alert apps need written approval ([operating agreement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement)).
- **API access.** The old product API (PA-API 5) is deprecated. The Creators API that replaces it needs 10 sales in the past 30 days ([Amazon](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/introduction)).
- **Rate cards.** Rates are now per account, behind a login. Adweek reported unannounced cuts of up to 50% for some publishers from late 2025 ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/amazon-associates-affiliate-rate-cuts-publishers/)).

**Flipkart**
- The official page lists mobiles up to 5%, computers up to 6% and cameras up to 4% (undated).
- Direct sign-ups have been paused since May 2018 ([Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/flipkart-affiliate-registration-not-working-heres-a-reliable-alternative/)).
- Through Cuelinks it pays up to 7.2%, with a 30-minute cookie ([Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/flipkart-affiliate-program)).

**Cuelinks**
- A script or WordPress plugin converts outbound links into affiliate links automatically ([plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/cuelinks)).
- Rates:
  - Myntra up to 9% (7.5% new users, 3.75% existing; 1-day cookie; no Telegram promotion).
  - Ajio up to 10.8%.
  - Tata CLiQ up to 5.4%.
  - Nykaa up to 5.04%.
  - boAt up to 7.2%.
  - Pepperfry up to ₹315 a sale.
  - Agoda up to 5.4%.
  - Reliance Digital is paused by the advertiser.

**EarnKaro**
- Rates are set per retailer, for example Noise at a flat 4% ([EarnKaro](https://earnkaro.com/blog/gonoise-affiliate-program/)).
- Earnings are confirmed in 30–90 days.

**Credit cards**
- vCommission: IndusInd ₹1.4K, IDFC ₹1.02K, AU ₹441 per card ([vCommission](https://www.vcommission.com/blog/publishers/festive-cpa-opportunities-high-paying-credit-card-campaigns-for-affiliates/)).
- INRDeals: Axis up to ₹2,300, IDFC up to ₹1,200. Unverified: the INRDeals pages returned 403 when rechecked, so the report leaves these out.

**Croma (via vCommission)**
- Phones ₹29.5–59.5 an order; laptops, audio and wearables 2.08%.
- Content sites are not allowed ([affi.io](https://affi.io/m/croma)).

**Travel**
- MakeMyTrip: hotels only, ₹140 domestic / ₹280 international, 1-day cookie.
- Booking.com closed its direct program on 20 Jun 2025 and moved to Awin, at about 4%.
- Agoda via Cuelinks: up to 5.4%.

## Other revenue

**Brand-paid launch coverage**
- 91mobiles shows brands will pay for it.
- Agency estimates for 2026: micro creators get ₹8K–80K per Reel. A 500K-subscriber tech channel gets ₹2.5–6 lakh per mid-roll and ₹5–12 lakh per dedicated review ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/influencer-marketing-pricing-india-2026/)).

**Chrome extensions.** Since Jun 2025 an extension may add an affiliate code only when it is disclosed, the user acts, and there is a real discount ([Chrome](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-policy-update-affiliate-ads-2025)).

## Rules

- **ASCI influencer guidelines.** Disclose any material connection up front (#ad, #sponsored and similar).
- **CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines (30 Nov 2023).** Bans 13 patterns, including false urgency, disguised ads and bait and switch. A Jun 2025 advisory told platforms to self-audit ([Lok Sabha answer, 25 Mar 2026](https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/PMS/writereaddata/2026_LS_B_5415.pdf)).
- **Online reviews standard (IS 19000:2022).** Still voluntary as of Mar 2026.
- **Section 194R.** 10% TDS applies when review units or other benefits a reviewer keeps exceed ₹20,000 a year.
