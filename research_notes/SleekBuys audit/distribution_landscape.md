# Distribution landscape for an Indian tech-deals site (Oct 2026)

## Google Search

**Updates.**
- Core updates in 2025–26: Mar, Jun–Jul and Dec 2025, then Mar–Apr and May–Jun 2026.
- Spam updates: Aug–Sep 2025, then Mar, Jun, Aug and Sep–Oct 2026 ([Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/products/rGHU1u87FJnkP6W2GwMi/history)).
- A Discover-only core update ran on 5 Feb 2026 ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/02/discover-core-update)).

**Policies.**
- **Scaled content abuse** covers "using generative AI tools… to generate many pages without adding value for users".
- **Thin affiliation** covers copied merchant descriptions.
- Both are in Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

**What still wins.** Google's [reviews guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews), updated Dec 2025, asks for:
- your own photos, video and measurements;
- comparisons with rivals;
- pros and cons;
- links to more than one seller;
- "best" picks backed by first-hand evidence;
- disclosed affiliate links.

**Studies on AI answers and clicks**

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| [Pew, Jul 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) | People clicked a result in 8% of visits when an AI summary showed, vs 15% without one |
| [Ahrefs, Feb 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/) | Position-1 click-through down 58% on keywords with AI Overviews (Dec 2025 vs Dec 2023) |
| [BrightEdge, 27 Nov 2025](https://www.brightedge.com/resources/weekly-ai-search-insights/black-friday-2024-vs-2025-what-a-year-of-testing-taught-google-about-ai-overviews) | AI Overviews on **83% of "best [product]" queries** (5% in 2024), 13% of "buy X" queries; electronics 9% → 24% |
| [Chartbeat via PPC Land, Apr 2026](https://ppc.land/small-publishers-lost-60-of-search-traffic-as-ai-reshapes-the-web/) | Search referrals down **60% for small publishers** (1,000–10,000 page views a day), 47% for medium and 22% for large ones, over two years to Feb 2026; Discover page views down 15% in 2025 |

**India**
- AI Mode in India has answered shopping queries with its own comparison tables since Apr 2026 ([Google India](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/explore-communicate/new-ways-google-is-using-ai-to-make-shopping-easier/)).
- Google began testing a Flipkart "Buy" button in Gemini and AI Mode on 26 Sep 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/26/google-tests-buying-from-walmart-owned-flipkart-through-gemini-and-ai-mode-in-india/)).
- Readers in India can choose "Preferred Sources" for Top Stories, live since Aug 2025 ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-preferred-sources-rolling-out-in-us-and-india-460631)).

## YouTube

**Big Indian tech channels, Sep 2026** (Social Blade and similar):
- Technical Guruji ~23.7M subscribers.
- Trakin Tech ~15.5–16M.
- Tech Burner ~12.5M.
- Beebom 3.6M.
- Geekyranjit ~3.3M.
- TechWiser ~2.5M.
- 91mobiles ~1.52M.

**Sale-season titles.** Small channels use the same formula: "Top 5 Best Phone Under 20000 in Flipkart Big Billion Days & Amazon GIF Sale 2026" (e.g. [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGRSc-m2Zqs), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dguXFZxdp3s)).

**YouTube Shopping affiliate in India**
- **Partners.** Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Purplle, Shopsy and Tira, with Amazon newly added. AJIO, Meesho, Snapdeal and Tata CLiQ are coming.
- **Uptake.** Over 50% of eligible Indian creators are enrolled, and payouts rose more than 160% year on year from Q1 2025 to Q1 2026 ([FoneArena, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/493334/youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-india-expansion.html)).
- **Eligibility.** YouTube Partner Program membership and at least 500 subscribers ([Storyboard18, 30 Mar 2026](https://storyboard18.com/digital/youtube-opens-shopping-affiliate-program-to-creators-with-500-subscribers-93649.htm)).
- **Clicks.** Shopping tags get more than twice the clicks of description links ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/youtube-brings-amazon-shopping-affiliate-to-india-as-it-unveils-new-ai-creator-and-commerce-tools-ws-o-111261.htm)).
- **Demand.** More than 450M people in India use YouTube for shopping searches.
- **Policy risk.** Since 15 Jul 2025 the "inauthentic content" rule removes mass-produced or repetitive channels from monetization ([SEJ](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-targets-mass-produced-content-in-monetization-update/550337/)).

## Instagram and Facebook (India)

**Audience** ([NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/instagram-users-in-india/), Sep 2026):
- Instagram ~532–555M, 39% aged 25–34.
- Facebook ~685M.

**Originality rules**
- Facebook has demoted repeat re-posters since Jul 2025.
- Since 30 Apr 2026, Instagram removes recommendations for accounts that mostly re-post. Credits and watermarks don't count as original ([Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)).

**Links**
- Shoppable Reels have been live in India since Apr 2026: up to 30 product links per Reel, affiliate links allowed ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/instagram-shoppable-reels-launch-what-it-means-for-creator-monetisation-in-india-94936.htm)).
- Up to 5 links in bio, and Story link stickers.
- Comment-to-DM tools such as ManyChat.

**Meta ad costs in India.** Median CPM about **$1.35** and median CPC about **$0.10**, Jul 2025–Jul 2026 ([Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/india)).
