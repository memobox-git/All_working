Data checked: 15 October 2026. Labels used throughout:
- **VERIFIED** = pulled directly from YouTube on 2026-10-15 with yt-dlp (channel subscriber count from the channel page; per-video upload date and views from each video's metadata; Shorts/long-form counts and view totals from the channel's Videos/Shorts/Live tabs).
- **CLAIMED** = the creator's own statement (video title, bio, interview, own website).
- **ESTIMATED** = my inference (e.g., "time to 100K" from the date of a milestone video, cadence computed from dated sample points, or YouTube's coarse "N years ago" relative dates).
- Social Blade returned HTTP 403 and the Wayback Machine was unreachable, so no third-party subscriber-history curves were available. Time to 100K therefore comes from dated milestone videos (1K/10K/100K thank-you, plaque-unboxing and Q&A uploads) or creator statements. Transcripts were also blocked (YouTube bot check / HTTP 429), so creator quotes come only from web sources and from two transcripts a parallel researcher had already downloaded (Ali Abdaal × Jeff Su; Avery Smith × Zach Wilson).
- View counts are lifetime counts as of 2026-10-15. They are not the views at the time of breakout.

## Q1. Which channels in this niche grew fastest in 2022–2026, and what were their numbers?

### Takeaway
No channel in the pure data-engineering or data-career niche verifiably went from 0 to 100K in 6–12 months. The fastest verified cases took about 16–18 months: Data with Baraa (~18 months, driven by marathon full courses) and Marina Wyss (~16 months, one AI/ML career video a week). The one sub-12-month case is adjacent: Nate Herk's AI-automation channel (a former BI analyst) passed a claimed 230K in 9 months and 1M in 24 months. Channels that went "0 to big" in 2025–2026 (CodeHead, Maddy Zhang, Aishwarya Srinivasan's relaunch) did it either with a very high posting cadence (2–5 long-form videos a week, or ~5 Shorts a week) or by attaching credentials and career framing to AI-engineering explainers.

## Q4. What did these creators say about their routine, team, batching and posting cadence?

### Takeaway
Direct creator statements are sparse because transcripts were blocked. The available evidence points one way:
- Most fast growers kept full-time big-tech jobs for a long time (Sundas still at Google; Marina until Sept 2026; Jeff Su for years).
