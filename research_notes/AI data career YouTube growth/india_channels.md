# Fast-growing India-focused data and AI career YouTube channels (case studies, checked 15 Oct 2026)

**How the numbers were collected (read this first).**
- **VERIFIED** means pulled from YouTube with yt-dlp 2026.08.19 on 15 Oct 2026. Subscriber counts come from `channel_follower_count`, which YouTube rounds to 3 significant figures. Full video and Shorts lists come from each channel's /videos and /shorts tabs. Exact upload dates and view counts come from per-video lookups (android player client) on a sample of videos: the oldest 5, every 3rd–15th video counting from the oldest, the top videos, and milestone videos.
- Video counts are public uploads only, so deleted, private and members-only videos are missing.
- **Shorts** dates are only available where sampled.
- **Approximate dates** come from YouTube's "N years ago" text. They are only month-accurate within the last 12 months, so they are used only for "last ~11 months" cadence.
- **CLAIMED** means stated by the creator or their company, for example in a channel description, a website or a press release.
- **THIRD-PARTY** means a tracker snapshot (vidIQ, DevEducators, SponsorRadar, NoxInfluencer).
- **ESTIMATED** means my inference.
- **Social Blade** returned HTTP 403 to both WebFetch and curl.
- **Wayback Machine** snapshots exist for several channel pages: Rishabh Mishra 23 Jun 2023, Ansh Lamba 26 Nov 2024, Akber Shaikh 26 May 2025 and Darshil Parmar Jan 2023/Jan 2024/Jun 2025, listed via the archive.org availability API. They could not be downloaded because the egress proxy reset the connection to web.archive.org.
- **Subscriber history:** as a result, no month-by-month subscriber history was obtainable. Time-to-100K is given only where a dated milestone video exists, and otherwise as a proxy.

## Q1. Which India-focused data/AI career channels grew fastest in 2022–2026, and what are their numbers?

### Takeaway
None of the channels has a verified 0→100K in 6–12 months, because no subscriber history was reachable. The fastest data/AI career channels on the evidence are:
- **Rishabh Mishra** (Hindi data analytics): 594K in about 4.6 years. A 10.1M-view Excel course went up in month 10.
- **Ansh Lamba** (English data engineering full courses): 158K in 23.4 months, with zero Shorts and exactly one upload a week.
- **Akber Shaikh** (Hinglish/English tech-career roadmaps): 126K in 17.6 months.
- **Satish Dhawale's personal channel** (Hindi Excel/Power BI/SQL): 405K in about 33 months, with an existing creator network behind it.
- **CampusX** (Hindi DS/GenAI): took about 4.25 years to reach 100K, then added roughly 500K subscribers in the two years after it pivoted to GenAI and Agentic AI. This is the strongest verified "AI content acceleration" case.
