Research date: 15 October 2026. "Checked 15 Oct 2026" means I fetched the vendor's own pricing or help page that day. Third-party figures carry the source's own date where one was given. INR prices appear only where a source gave them. I did not convert USD to INR, because I found no verified October 2026 exchange rate.

Context the report writer must keep in mind:
- TikTok has been banned in India since June 2020 and remained banned as of a July 2026 source, with no confirmed relaunch. Any "cross-post to TikTok" advice only applies to a US-facing account run lawfully from outside India. See the Cross-posting section.
- CapCut is still blocked in India as of October 2026. See the Editing section.
- Stripe has been invite-only for new Indian businesses since May 2024. This matters for any tool that collects payments only through Stripe. See the Funnel section.

## Idea research and outlier finding (vidIQ, TubeBuddy, 1of10, Spotter Studio, OutlierKit, YouTube Inspiration/Research tab, Google Trends, Reddit, AnswerThePublic)

### Takeaway
The cheapest useful setup is free: YouTube Studio's Inspiration tab, plus 1of10 Free (60 credits a month, 5 outlier results), plus vidIQ Free (150 AI credits a month). Add one paid outlier tool once Shorts go daily. 1of10 Basic costs $15 a month billed yearly, and vidIQ Boost costs $199 a year. Spotter Studio may not be usable from India, and YouTube's AI "Ideas/Outlines" excluded India at launch.

Boost gives 2,000 AI credits a month, unlimited trends research, thumbnail generation and personalized ideas. Max gives 6,000 credits a month and "up to 11 hours of short clipping and generation". Both Boost and Max are "billed yearly", but the official page did not render dollar amounts. — [vidIQ pricing](https://vidiq.com/pricing/)

### Takeaway
CapCut is still blocked in India as of October 2026, so Chris cannot rely on it from India. Use Descript as the core editor. For phone work in India the usual substitutes are Instagram Edits (free, no watermark), VN (free, no forced watermark), YouTube Create, Adobe Premiere mobile (free), and DaVinci Resolve free on desktop.

### Cited Findings
- **CapCut, India status (checked 11 Oct 2026):**
  - "CapCut is still not officially available in India in 2026". It is absent from the Indian Play Store and App Store, and its cloud and AI features are region-locked.
  - The blocking order dates to 29 Jun 2020 under IT Act Section 69A. CapCut was not named on the June 2020 list but is unavailable in practice as a ByteDance product.
  - On 21 May 2026 CapCut announced a Gemini partnership. ABP Live reported that Indian users will likely miss out because "CapCut's backend services remain blocked here."

- **CapCut, a second India report (checked 8 Oct 2026):** Still blocked; the website does not load in India; no official reversal. Pre-ban installs can still do basic offline editing, but server features (cloud, templates, AI) fail. A VPN does not change CapCut's legal status in India. — [VideoWizardTools](https://videowizardtools.com/why-is-capcut-banned-in-india/); [DICloak](https://dicloak.com/blog-detail/is-capcut-banned-in-india-in-2026-what-users-need-to-know)

## Thumbnails and titles (Canva, Photoshop, ThumbnailTest, YouTube Test & Compare)

### Takeaway
YouTube's native Test & Compare is free and now tests both titles and thumbnails, up to 3 variants on long-form videos, with the winner chosen by watch time. That makes paid A/B tools optional. Use Canva (Pro about ₹4,500 a year in India, per a May 2026 source) for design.

### Cited Findings
