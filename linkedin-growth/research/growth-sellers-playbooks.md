## 2.0 Consolidated playbook: 15 rules most of these sellers agree on (details and sources in 2.1-2.11)
1. **Own one topic.** One main topic plus 2-3 subtopics, with about 80% of posts inside them.
   - LinkedIn's 360Brew ranking now distributes by topic: RvdB's scan found same-topic peers' posts +42% and clients' posts −56%. "360Brew remembers your last 1,000 actions."
   - Sources: RvdB ep. 307 and his posts; Kevin Box on Chris Donnelly; Alicia Teltz ("persona-based distribution").
2. **The hook is the first 1-2 lines, and it must be specific.**
   - Line 1 is under about 100 characters (Vedika), 8-10 words (Lara), or one line (Jasmin). Line 2 is a "re-hook" (Lara) or a value promise (Unnati).
   - Lead with a result, a mistake, or a controversy (Vedika). Spend about 25% of writing time on the hook (Adam Robinson).
   - Numbers in hooks: 52% of Matt Barker's posts use them, vs 22% for the average creator (MagicPost).
3. **Structure:** Hook → Re-hook → Lead → Body → Power-ending → CTA (Lara). Or HIT: Hook, Insight from real experience with numbers, one Takeaway (Vedika). Or Unnati's 6 layers: Contradiction hook → Credibility stack → Value promise → Specific data → One-line takeaway → Visual proof.
4. **Format for skimming.** Paragraphs of 3 lines at most. Lists use numbers, one-liners, and 7 items as the "magic number" (Jasmin). Short-short-long-short rhythm (Vedika). Write like you talk; delete AI-slop phrases (Vedika, TGS).
5. **Put a native visual on almost every post,** ideally an infographic, cheat sheet or real photo. Vedika: 70% image, 20% carousel, 10% graphics. RvdB: text+image is the most saveable; "avoid selfies"; use carousels selectively (not daily under 10k followers).
6. **Cadence:** 2-5 strong posts a week, never two within 24 hours.
   - RvdB: 2-4 a week is now optimal. Charlie Hills: back-to-back posts lose up to 20% reach.
   - Growth sprints were daily: Vedika at 0-100k, Adam Robinson, Peter Yang, Sam Browne.
   - Batch-write on the weekend (Vedika on Sunday; Peter Yang on Saturday; Chris Donnelly's "Attention Engine": 2 hours of batch writing).
7. **Work the first hour.** Reply to every comment within an hour (Vedika), and to the first 6-8 within minutes (Justin Welsh, per Vedika). Spend about 15 minutes after publishing (RvdB). Pin a comment or end with a P.S. question so the thread keeps going (Jasmin).
8. **Comment on others every day, with substance.**
   - CEA (Compliment, Expand, Ask) on 5-10 posts a day (Justin Welsh).
   - A 100-creator list: 1/4 bigger than you, 1/2 your size, 1/4 prospects (Charlie Hills).
   - 5-10 thoughtful comments on 2nd- and 3rd-degree posts (RvdB). Comments of 15+ words get a claimed 2.5x reach boost (Charlie Hills citing RvdB).
   - A great reply on a top creator's post can bring hundreds of followers (Peter Yang). Your post only reaches another creator's audience when they comment on it (Matt Barker).
9. **Grow the network on purpose.** 10-15 personalised ICP connection requests a day (Adam Robinson 10; Charlie Hills 15 via Sales Navigator). Vedika's 14k-in-2-months client added 400+ relevant people a month.
10. **Trend translation / news-jacking.** Set Google Alerts on niche keywords and be first to explain what breaking news means for your audience (Justin Welsh). In the verified data, day-one posts on big AI launches get a median 8.1 reactions per 1k followers, against 1.9 for niche or regional business news (section 4.4). RvdB 2024: trending topics get +50% reach.
11. **Repurpose one insight into 6 posts:** main insight, contrarian take, personal story, how-to, mistake to avoid, prediction (Justin Welsh). Turn a newsletter into a thread, then a carousel, then short posts, over 4-6 weeks.
12. **Use proof posts:** client wins and case studies with numbers and before/after (Vedika's client strategy; Matt Barker; TGS "Specific Data" layer). They convert reach into follows and leads.
13. **Lead magnets: put the value in the post; avoid "comment YES" bait.**
    - LinkedIn has refused since 2022 to promote posts that ask for likes or reactions. RvdB (Feb 2026) says comment-for-template bait is "actively down-ranked". RvdB 2024: gated content performs 90% worse.
    - Keep promotion to about 1 post in 5 (Vedika's 1-3-1 cycle; RvdB's "posting like a billboard").
    - In our data, comment-keyword posts get 3-4x the comments but about 40-65% fewer reactions than the same creator's other posts.
14. **Never use pods or automation.** LinkedIn's 13 Mar 2026 statement bans pods and automated comments. Penalties: reduced reach, account restrictions, removal from Top Voices. Organic peer groups (Matt Barker's WhatsApp group) and collaborations are fine: micro-interviews, joint posts, co-hosted sessions (Justin Welsh, per Vedika).
15. **Make the profile a landing page and measure.**
    - Headline in the first 100 characters (role, who you help, proof). Banner: what you do + who + proof. Featured: best posts and a lead magnet.
    - A LinkedIn newsletter: follow prompts convert about 60% of new followers (RvdB).
    - Log hook type and saves for 30 posts (Unnati). Each quarter, study the top 10% of posts (Matt Barker: 45% of impressions came from fewer than 10% of posts).

### 2.1 Vedika Bhaia (The Growth Square co-founder, now Founder of Social Capital Inc.)
Her own playbook post, "0-295k followers on LinkedIn. 4 years of data" (4,945 reactions, 472 comments, 2025-08-06) [P] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vedikabhaia_0-295k-followers-on-linkedin-4-years-of-activity-7358820787455213569-GXm8
- **Format mix:** image posts 70%, carousels 20%, graphics 10% (graphics only for data visualisation, never stock photos).
- **Image posts:** keep the hook under 100 characters, use personal stories, add data, use white space, end with a question.
- **Carousels:** open with the end result, 8-10 slides at most, one point per slide, a consistent design, actionable steps.
- **0-100k:** posted daily at 9:30 AM IST, plus 5 PM on alternate days. Replied to every comment within an hour. Engaged on 10 other posts a day.
- **100k-200k:** cut to 4-5 posts a week and started sharing client results. Content buckets. Found 5 PM was her best slot in analytics.
- **200k+:** 2-3 posts a week plus 1 video. Behind-the-scenes posts, documented failures, relationships built through DMs.
- **Process:** write the whole week on Sunday. Get ideas from Twitter, Instagram and podcasts. Train a Claude Project on your own posts, but only after you can write well yourself. **1 giveaway per week.**
- **Her reach ranking by post type:** personal stories > giveaways > case studies with data > how-to frameworks > industry insights > general advice.
- **What she says she never did:** engagement pods, comment farming, chasing trends, generic listicles, bought followers, clickbait hooks.
  - Contradiction: the same post says "without any growth hacks or giveaways" while also recommending a weekly giveaway. Many of her biggest posts are comment-keyword giveaways (see 2.11 and 4.4).

**HIT framework** (979 reactions) [P] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vedikabhaia_ive-written-800-linkedin-posts-in-the-last-activity-7394698283018973186-OQjI
- **H = Hook:** the first 3 lines should make people think "wait, what?". Admit you were wrong, share an unpopular opinion, or call out advice that doesn't work. "don't build up to it."
- **I = Insight from real experience:** real numbers and failures. She says posts with actual revenue numbers or exact timelines get 3x the engagement.
- **T = Takeaway:** one action the reader can take this week, or a reflective question. Not "what are your thoughts?" ("that's lazy").

**Anti-AI-slop writing rules** (2,015 reactions, 2026-01-06) [P] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vedikabhaia_5-years-ago-i-wrote-my-first-linkedin-post-activity-7414269325211054080-f2Ob
- Write the way you talk. She uses lowercase on purpose because it feels less formal.
- Lead with the result, the mistake or the controversy. Be specific.
- Rhythm: short line, short line, one longer line, then short again.
- Make it actionable: "do this, then this, then this."

**Algorithm notes from her LinkedIn NYC visit** [3P summary of her post] https://ascendants.in/spotlight/linkedin-algorithm-secrets-vedika-bhaia/
- The first 90 minutes decide a post's fate. She treats one comment as worth about 10 likes.
- Posting cadence: a 1-3-1 cycle of 1 personal post, 3 value posts and 1 promotional post. "If you're always selling, you get shadowbanned."
- Links in the post got her about 500 views versus 3,000+ in the first comment. Alternative: "DM me for the link."
- LinkedIn tests a new account's first 10 posts more widely.
- 1,200+ consecutive days of posting.

**Origin and business** [3P] https://methodtomadness.substack.com/p/how-vedika-bhaia-grew-her-linkedin
- Started in 2020 through a blog-growth challenge in a digital marketing course. Her first week of posts got about 500 likes and 50k impressions.
- A 15-person workshop led to her first client, which became The Growth Square.
- 6 posts a week. Old posts are repurposed months later with new creatives. Reels are batch-recorded (30 in 3 days).
- Agency clients pay about $1,000 a month or more.
- Crack Those Socials academy: about 1,300 paid students, 70-80% of them from her LinkedIn.
- Recognised as LinkedIn Influencer of the Year 2023, and 2025 per her own post.

### 4.4 What the patterns say
1. **Aphorism one-liners have the highest median** (2,336 reactions; 3,061 in the last 12 months). But it is a big-account game: Justin Welsh and Sahil Bloom both have 700k-900k followers, and their audiences come for wisdom posts. Per 1k followers they score 5.1-5.7: good, but not the best.
2. **News-jacking splits in two:**
   - **Day-one posts on big, widely discussed AI launches** are among the best per follower. 8 posts (Claude Opus 5.5 / Claude skills, Nano Banana 2, Gemini 3, GeoSpy) have a median of **1,847 reactions and 8.1 per 1k followers** (Charlie Hills, Ruben Hassid, John Peslar). Big-name borrowed authority does well too: Sam Altman × Nikhil Kamath podcast (Vedika, 3,018), "LinkedIn is literally telling you…" (Jasmin, 2,693).
   - **Niche or regional business news** (Badshah's vodka, Mivi's phone, an Indian offline-AI launch, Favikon's ranking) gets a median of **248 reactions and 1.9 per 1k** (6 posts by Unnati, Mishka and Vaibhav).
   - Overall the pattern sits at 4.7 per 1k (22 posts). The lever is speed plus a famous subject plus a practical "here's how to use it" body. This is Justin Welsh's "trend translation" and Vedika's client playbook ("trending topics + fresh angle"). **It is the most transferable lever for a data & AI creator.**
3. **Milestones (6.0 per 1k overall; 6.4 in the last 12 months) and then-vs-now arcs (5.0-5.1)** work when the milestone is real: an award, Forbes 30u30, "0-295k". The client's 25k moment and a "13k → 25k in 60 days" story are natural uses.
4. **Listicles and contrarian takes have slipped.** Across all 225 posts they score 3.8 and 2.9 per 1k; in the last 12 months only 2.5 and 1.7. This fits RvdB's Feb 2026 warning about "low-value listicles with zero depth" and Saywhat's finding that generic "5 lessons" posts lost value. Listicles still work when they carry real proof (Eric Partaker's "after hiring over 2000 people") or ride a hot topic ("Claude Code has 93 commands. Here are the 16 worth learning first", 2,072).
5. **Vulnerable confessions are high-variance:** median 724, but this pattern produced Lara's 6,967, her top post. Use them rarely and only with a real photo or a specific moment.
6. **Credential-first hooks** ("I have 300k followers & get 80+ leads…", "My company hit $20M ARR…") have a low reaction median (654) but a high comment ratio (0.36). They pull leads more than reach.
7. **Question hooks and warning/FOMO hooks are weakest** (0.9 and 1.2 per 1k in the last 12 months).
8. **Comment-keyword lead magnets trade reactions for comments.** Their comments-to-reactions ratio is 1.63, against 0.2-0.4 for everything else. Per 1k followers they score lowest (0.6) over the last 12 months. Same-creator comparison (leadmag.py):
   - **Vedika:** 10 comment-keyword posts at median 1,077 reactions and 690 comments, against 26 other posts at 1,764 reactions and 170 comments.
   - **Chris Donnelly:** 3 posts at 751 reactions and 1,880 comments, against 9 others at 2,175 and 511.
   - Lead magnets grow DMs and email lists, but they don't win on reactions. They carry the 2026 "engagement bait" risk RvdB describes (no official LinkedIn rule found).
9. **Image memes for an in-group** (Daniel Murray's marketer memes) get the most reactions per follower (15.7 per 1k) but almost no comments (0.03 ratio). Good for reach, weak for conversation. A data-engineering meme now and then could do the same job.

## 7. What this means for the client (about 13k → 25k in about 60 days), from the evidence above
- **Required pace:** about 12k net new followers in 60 days, or about 200 a day.
- **Benchmarks for that pace:**
  - Vedika's agency client gained 14k in 2 months, with "7-8 back to back bangers".
  - TGS's finance creator added 10k+ in 55 days.
  - A Matt Barker client at about 18k gained about 2k from one carousel.
  - Sam Browne gained 12k in 24 hours from one 11k-reaction carousel (2022, old algorithm).
