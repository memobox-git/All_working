# Who we checked, and which rule each one is behind

Every number below was read from that person's public LinkedIn page on Oct 14, 2026. "Typical post" is the median reactions on their most recent posts, where their profile could be read. In total we checked **1,099 posts with 100+ reactions from 305 creators**; every post is in [`data/verified-posts.csv`](data/verified-posts.csv). This page lists the people the rules actually rest on.

## My assumptions (not from any person or dataset)

- The **17-19k floor** by mid-December.
- The per-lever targets (1-2k followers from your own audiences, 300+ registrants per event, and so on).
- The **$1.50-per-follower** cutoff for paid boosts.
- That these patterns will hold for *your* account. They're correlations from other people's hits, not experiments. Your own "followers gained" numbers, reviewed every Sunday, are what confirm or kill each rule.
