# Hook swipe file: verified LinkedIn hooks from data, AI and LinkedIn-growth creators

Every hook below comes from a public LinkedIn post that was fetched and checked between Oct 13 and Oct 14, 2026. Each post has **100 or more reactions**. Reaction counts are exact as of the fetch. Impressions are private on LinkedIn; at a typical 1% reaction rate, 100 reactions works out to roughly 10,000 impressions.

- Verified posts with 100+ reactions: **644** (AI: 367, LinkedIn growth: 164, Data: 113)
- Creators represented: **200**
- `R/1k` = reactions per 1,000 followers. It lets you compare a 20k-follower creator with a 900k one. A hook that beats its author's audience size is the real signal, so read that column before the raw count.

How to use it: don't copy a hook word for word. Take the pattern (the first column of the table below), then fill it with your own true story, company, number or opinion.
