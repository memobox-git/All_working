## 2. Collaboration targets

Charlie Hills' rule for an engagement list: about 1/4 bigger than you, 1/2 your size, 1/4 prospects. People who sell the same DE course as Stackle are unlikely partners, so these lean toward *adjacent* audiences and peers. Check fit and recent activity before reaching out.

| Who (followers) | Why them | The concrete ask |
