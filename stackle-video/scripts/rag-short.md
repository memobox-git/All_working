# RAG, as a talking-head Short (draft for review)

**Format:** the style of Vaibhav Sisinty's Shorts (reference: "Your Slow Internet Isn't What You Think… Fix It With AI!", youtube.com/shorts/tMSqFrAiggw), with AI engineering topics. You on camera carry the video, and the graphics only support you.

**Length:** about 140 spoken words, so 50 to 55 seconds. Vertical 1080×1920 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

**Status:** script for review. A mockup of the look is in `stackle-video/graphics/rag-short-mockup.jpg`. The real thing needs your footage (see "Filming" below).

---

## What makes the reference work

| Element | In the reference | For us |
|---|---|---|
| Presenter | Him, waist up, talking straight to the lens, in a warm, softly lit room with shelves, plants and lamps behind him | You, the same framing. A real room with depth, not a plain wall |
| Pace | Jump cuts between sentences, with no pauses left in. The framing alternates between wide and a tighter punch-in | The same: every pause cut, a punch-in (about 112%) on every other sentence, and a slow push-in on the hook |
| Captions | Small white text on a dark rounded pill, 2 to 5 words at a time, sitting mid-frame at chest height | The same. Plain, no colour pops, so they don't fight your face |
| Props | One glossy 3D object at a time (a cartoon robot holding a Wi-Fi sign) popping in near his hands on the key line | One 3D prop per idea, about every 8 to 10 seconds: a robot librarian for RAG, an open book, a database. Made with AI image generation and cut out |
| Sound | His voice, clean and close | Your voice. **No music bed**, or a very quiet ambient one if you want, about 25 dB under the voice. A soft pop when a prop lands |
| Hook | A surprising claim about something you use every day | "Your company's AI chatbot isn't dumb…" |

## Script

Each line is one cut. **[prop]** marks where a prop pops in, and **[card]** marks a small clean diagram card.

| # | You say (to camera) | On screen |

## Filming (about 10 minutes)

- **Camera:** your phone, vertical, 4K at 30 fps, rear camera, on a tripod at eye level, about 1.2 to 1.5 m away. Frame yourself from the waist up, with your eyes about a third of the way down the frame.
- **Room:** put some depth behind you, 2 to 3 m of shelves, plants or a lamp switched on. Face a window or a soft light.
- **Sound:** a clip-on mic if you have one. Otherwise keep the phone close and the room quiet (no fan or AC).
- **Clothes:** a plain, solid colour (no small patterns), darker than the background.
- **Delivery:** say each line 2 or 3 times, with a short pause in between. Don't restart when you stumble; I pick the cleanest take of each line, like the Senior to Staff cut. A teleprompter app on the phone helps.
- **Send:** the file, or put it in Descript and share the project.

## The edit (done here, from your footage)

1. Transcribe the footage and pick the best take of each line.
2. Cut every pause, and alternate wide and punch-in framing by line.
3. Add the captions: white text on a dark pill, 2 to 5 words at a time, mid-frame.
4. Pop in the props and cards on their words, each with a soft shadow and a small bounce.
5. Mix the sound: your voice cleaned up and levelled for Shorts (−14 LUFS), with a soft pop on each prop and no music.
6. Render vertical 1080×1920, plus an `.srt` file.
