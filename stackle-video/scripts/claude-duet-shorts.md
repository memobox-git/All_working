# Claude Duet: five vertical Shorts

Five 1080x1920 Shorts for TikTok and YouTube Shorts. They are cut from the 8-minute voice-over (`claude-duet-8min-voiceover.md`), with burned-in captions and an end card that points to the full video. Every Short is under 3 minutes, so YouTube treats each one as a Short.

Built from `stackle-video/source/duet`: `prep/shorts.py` cuts the voice, `shorts.html?s=<id>` is the scene, and `music.py work/shorts/<id>` makes the soundtrack.

| File | Length | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| `claude-duet-short-1-in-a-minute.mp4` | 0:50 | What Duet is and the three kinds of work it does |
| `claude-duet-short-2-vs.mp4` | 1:11 | Duet vs Grok Bot, Muse and Dots, and my take |
| `claude-duet-short-3-safety.mp4` | 0:54 | Approvals, what is blocked, passwords, payments, prompt injection |
| `claude-duet-short-4-catch.mp4` | 0:56 | Usage limits, the one-time reset, outside the US, the Cowork version |
| `claude-duet-short-5-six-parts.mp4` | 1:32 | The six parts of every AI agent, using Duet as the example |

Replace `[full video link]` with the long video's URL once it is live.

## Posting notes

- Post the Shorts a day or two apart, not all at once. Start with Short 1, then Short 2.
- On YouTube, link each Short to the long video with the "Related video" field, so viewers can tap through to the full breakdown.
- TikTok captions do not make links clickable, so the TikTok captions name the channel instead of linking it.
- The captions are burned into the picture, so turn off TikTok's auto-captions to avoid two sets on screen.
