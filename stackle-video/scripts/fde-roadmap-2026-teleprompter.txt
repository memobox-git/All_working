A forward deployed engineer, or FDE, is a software developer who works inside one customer's business to make a product work there.
Most developers build one product for thousands of users and rarely meet any of them.
An FDE sits with a single client, learns how its people work, and writes the code that connects the software to their data, their systems, and their daily tasks.
