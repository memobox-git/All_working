## What each one covers (from chapters and descriptions)

- **Aishwarya Srinivasan (#2).** This is the most viewed recent roadmap. The hook says most people go about it "backwards" by collecting certificates. The order is: core PM skills (6 to 8 weeks), AI basics (4 to 6 weeks), product intuition for AI (3 to 4 weeks), shipping a tiny AI product (6 to 8 weeks), then MLOps and evals (3 to 4 weeks), then getting visible. The total is 4 to 5 months at 10 to 15 hours a week. Evals come after the build.
- **HelloPM course (#1).** This is a deep course on LLM internals, including tokenization, training stages, transformers and GPUs. It also covers context engineering, RAG, prompting, fine-tuning and agents, with an AI note-taker case study.
- **HelloPM roadmap (#8).** It covers three types of AI PM, a skill map, a proof-of-work portfolio, "talk to users, research, then build", the job hunt as a numbers game, and a "three bucket" strategy.
- **Aakash Gupta (#4).** It covers AI PM pay in the US and India. It also explains why jobs are not titled "AI PM", predictive versus generative AI, prompts, fine-tuning and RAG, why evals are still needed, agents and MCP, the portfolio, and seven next steps.
- **Neel Sikdar (#3, #6).** Video #3 is mostly a general PM roadmap: skills, tools, soft skills, internships, side projects, LinkedIn and interviews. Video #6 is a month-by-month 90-day plan with "no MBA, no coding" in the title.
- **codebasics (#11).** This is the largest data channel in the set, with 1.58 million subscribers. It is a panel that covers reports, interview tips, the skill gap, understanding data, salary (a chapter claims a "30-40% extra salary for AI PM"), a career-switch playbook and portfolio assignments.
- **Dr. Nancy Li (#7).** It covers four ways in with no experience, including moving from data scientist or engineer to AI PM.
- **Lenny's Podcast (#5).** It covers AI PM roles, getting started, the portfolio, and what separates the top 5% of AI PMs.

## What all of them cover (and the script covers too)

AI basics (LLMs, RAG, agents, fine-tuning, prompting), evals, a portfolio of built projects, interview prep, and the idea that you do not need an MBA.

## Where they are wrong, out of date, or disagree

- **The "no coding" claim.** Videos #6 and #10 put "no coding" in the title. A senior AI PM job ad posted this month requires a 60-minute coding test, and video #2 says the technical bar has gone up. The script takes the middle ground: you do not need to become an engineer, but you should be able to read simple code and build small things.
- **Old videos with 2026 in the title.** Video #7 was uploaded in April 2023 and video #9 in June 2025, and both have 2026 in the title. Video #3 is mainly a general PM roadmap.
- **Time to get hired.** The claims are 90 days (#6), 4 to 5 months (#2), about 6 months (Aakash Gupta's newsletter) and 12 months (Product School's written roadmap).
- **The order of evals.** Video #2 teaches evals after building the first product. The script teaches evals first, so every portfolio project has a quality score.
- **Unverified salary claim.** The "30-40% extra salary for AI PM" claim in video #11 could not be checked. The posted AI PM base ranges below sit close to the general PM base median of $185,000 on Levels.fyi, so this sample does not show a flat 30 to 40% premium. This is not an exact comparison, because ads and self-reported pay are different data.
- **Too deep for the job.** Video #1 spends a long time on transformers, GPUs and training stages. The job ads read for this video ask about model behaviour, evals and trade-offs, not model internals.

## Gaps, and the angle the script takes

1. **Pay by level from real job ads.** The script uses ranges posted from August to October 2026, plus the Levels.fyi total-pay median, and says clearly which numbers are base pay and which are total pay.
2. **A pass or fail test at the end of every phase.** None of the chapter lists show one.
3. **Evals before building, with the reason given out loud.**
4. **Data and SQL as a named skill, with a concrete query and an A/B test.** Video #11 touches on "understanding data". The script makes it something you can do.
5. **Real job titles to search for.** Video #4 explains why the title "AI PM" is rare. The script adds two real titles from the job ads.

## Changes made to the script after this research

- **The second line of the hook.** It used to be about certificates, which was the same idea as the hook in video #2. It now says the roadmap is built on what real job ads ask for.
- **The top-lab salary line.** It came from a recruiter's tracker. It was replaced with the Levels.fyi total-pay median, which comes from 4,972 submissions.
- **Phase three.** It now says why evals come before building.
- **Phase five.** It now tells the viewer to search more than one job title, and names two real titles.
- **Phase one.** One sentence was cut to keep the length about the same.
