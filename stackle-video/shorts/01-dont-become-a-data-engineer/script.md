# Short 01: Don't become a data engineer if…

**Shot:** 2, a numbered list with a fixed top title. It copies Sundas Khalid's "Don't Become a Data Analyst if" (3.5M views).
**Words:** 139 · **Length:** about 56 seconds at 150 words a minute
**Needs:** Chris alone. One filming session of about 10 minutes, including the extra clips.

**What they all do:** the title says the topic from the first frame. Each reason is "if you don't like X". About 60 seconds.

**Our angle:** the reasons come from ten years of doing the job, and they are the parts a job ad leaves out. Each reason has one concrete moment the viewer can picture. The Short ends with a question that pulls comments.

## Filming

- **Phone:** vertical, on a tripod at chest height, about 1 metre away. Frame from just above your head to your chest.
- **Mic:** the podcast mic on a desk stand, in the frame at one side.
- **Light:** a window beside you, or one soft light at 45 degrees. A real room with some depth behind you.
- **Delivery:** one take, looking into the lens. If you trip on a line, pause and say the whole sentence again.

**Extra clips** (3 seconds each, phone on the same tripod, just moved):

| Clip | What to film |
|---|---|
| A | A laptop showing a dashboard with an empty chart |
| B | A laptop showing a pipeline error: a red failed task, or an error message in a terminal |
| C | You at your desk from the side, typing |
| D | A documentation page for a new tool, scrolling |

## Edit

1. **Cuts:** remove every pause and every repeated take.
2. **Captions:** 1–3 words at a time, bold, white, with the key word in amber (#F59E0B). Place them at chest height, about 60% of the way down, above the point label.
3. **Overlays:** the files in `overlays/` are full-size 1080×1920 transparent images, so each one lines up when placed over the whole frame.
   - `title.png` runs for the whole Short.
   - `pill-1.png`, `pill-2.png` and `pill-3.png` each run only during their point (times above).
4. **Pace:** about 2 picture changes every 10 seconds, counting zooms and clips. Calm is fine: the competitor Shorts that did best changed picture about 1.5 times every 10 seconds.
5. **Sound:** even out the volume. No music.
6. **Export:** one 1080×1920 file, used on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
