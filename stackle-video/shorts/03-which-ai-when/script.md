**Needs:** Chris alone, one take of about a minute. Same setup as Short 02.

**What they all cover:** ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini side by side, mostly as "which $20 plan wins". The common split, which written comparisons agree with: ChatGPT for all-round everyday use, Claude for coding and long documents, Gemini for people who work in Google's apps, and Perplexity for answers with sources.

**Where they disagree or go out of date:** model names and prices change every few months, and the sources name different models for the same month. The script names no models and no prices.

**Our angle:** the others are 9 to 27 minutes long and set the tools up as a contest. This Short gives one job per tool in under a minute, and adds the two tools people already have at work (Gemini in Google's apps, Copilot in Microsoft's) and a tool for studying your own files (NotebookLM).

**Sources for every fact in the script:**

## Filming

- **Phone:** vertical, on a tripod at chest height, about 1 metre away, framed from just above your head to your chest.
- **Background:** a metre or more in front of the wall, so the cut-out effects stay clean.
- **Delivery:** one take, looking into the lens. Pause for a beat before each "Use…", because each tool's effect starts on its name. If you trip, pause and say the whole sentence again.
