# Short 03: Which AI to use when

**Shot:** a vertical video of about two minutes, edited like Short 02: a fixed title, a numbered label for each tool, captions one word at a time, a different effect for each tool, and music with sound effects.
**Words:** 866 · **Length:** about 5 minutes 46 seconds at 150 words a minute
**Needs:** Chris alone, one take of about three minutes, with pauses. Same setup as Short 02.
**Channel:** TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts (AI). Not for LinkedIn, where AI tool posts get 120 to 260 impressions.

---

**What they all cover:** ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini side by side, mostly as "which $20 plan wins". The common split, which written comparisons agree with: ChatGPT for all-round everyday use, Claude for coding and long documents, Gemini for people who work in Google's apps, and Perplexity for answers with sources.

**Where they disagree or go out of date:** model names and prices change every few months, and the sources name different models for the same month. The script names no models and no prices.

**Our angle:** the others are 9 to 27 minutes long and set the tools up as a contest. This Short gives one job per tool in under a minute, and adds the two tools people already have at work (Gemini in Google's apps, Copilot in Microsoft's) and a tool for studying your own files (NotebookLM).

**Sources for every fact in the script:**

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT makes images and handles everyday tasks | OpenAI's product pages; brandedagency.com and skyhoora.com 2026 comparisons |
| Claude is the usual pick for coding and long documents | clickforest.com (October 2026) and tech-insider.org comparisons |
| Gemini works inside Gmail and Docs and can summarise email threads | Google Workspace guides (gend.co, buildfastwithai.com); Google's 10 March 2026 Workspace update |
| Copilot works inside Word, Excel and Teams and can summarise meetings | Microsoft 365 Copilot overviews (xecu.net, maxsum.com, January 2026) |
| Perplexity searches the web and shows its sources | Perplexity's own FAQ ("How does Perplexity work?") |
| NotebookLM answers only from the files you upload | University guides (Siena, UC Davis, The New School) and Google's help pages |

## Filming

- **Phone:** vertical, on a tripod at chest height, about 1 metre away, framed from just above your head to your chest.
- **Background:** a metre or more in front of the wall, so the cut-out effects stay clean.
- **Delivery:** one take, looking into the lens. Pause for a beat before each "Use…", because each tool's effect starts on its name. If you trip, pause and say the whole sentence again.

## Script

Told the Storyworthy way. It opens inside one night with real pressure: a long report, a missed meeting, a full inbox, and one AI tab for all of it. Each tool then solves one of those jobs, and every part leads into the next with a reason ("the report is done, but you still missed that meeting"). It ends back on the same night, the opposite of how it began: the right tool for each job.

## For Chris to check

- **"Best at" is a judgement,** not a measured fact. It matches what the written comparisons agree on. Change any pairing that doesn't match how you use the tools.
- **Gemini in Gmail and Copilot in Word, Excel and Teams** need a paid plan for some features, so the script says "can".
- **NotebookLM's name:** one guide says Google now calls it Gemini Notebook. That comes from a single source and is not confirmed. Check the name in the app before filming.
- **Added by me:** the opening scene (ten at night, a report, a missed meeting, a full inbox) and the examples (a trip, an image, your notes). The scene is a "you" situation, not a story about you, so nothing in it claims something happened to you.
