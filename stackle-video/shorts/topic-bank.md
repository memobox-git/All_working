# Shorts topic bank

60 Shorts, about 4 weeks at 14 a week. Each topic uses one of the 12 shots in `stackle-video/research/shots/shot-list.md` and copies a competitor Short that already worked. View counts were checked on 16 October 2026 unless marked otherwise.

**W1** = already written in `week-01.md`.

**Needs:**
- **Solo:** Chris alone.
- **Guest:** one other person.
- **Meetup:** filmed at a Stackle meetup.
- **Prop:** a physical object.
- **Story:** only Chris can tell it.
- **Numbers:** needs sourced numbers before filming.
- **CHECK:** a hiring-side claim Chris must confirm.
