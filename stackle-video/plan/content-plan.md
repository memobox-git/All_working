# Stackle YouTube: what to build and how

Goal: 100,000 subscribers by 15 April 2027.
Output: 3 long videos and 14 Shorts a week, one free full course a month, a free bootcamp in month 3.
Content mix: 45% courses, builds and explainers · 35% AI and data careers · 20% AI stories tied to the viewer's job.

Every idea below copies a format that has already broken out on YouTube, or that already won on Chris's LinkedIn. The "Proof" column says which. Views were checked on YouTube on 15 October 2026. LinkedIn numbers are impressions from the stackle-linkedin-posts skill.

## 5. How we build it

### One folder per video

```
stackle-video/videos/01-start-over-data-engineer/
  package.md    3 titles, 3 thumbnail ideas, hook, description, pinned comment, links
  script.md     the full script (stackle-script skill, with its research brief)
  shorts.md     3–5 Shorts cut from the video + new Shorts for the week
  graphics/     on-screen graphics as HTML, rendered to MP4 with source/render.js
  linkedin.md   the LinkedIn post for launch day (stackle-linkedin-posts skill)
```

### The weekly loop

| Day | Claude | Chris | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Builds next week's 3 packages: 3 titles and 3 thumbnail ideas each | Picks and locks the titles and thumbnails | Designer starts thumbnails |
| Tuesday | Writes the 3 scripts and 14 Shorts scripts, with research briefs | Reviews the scripts | — |
| Wednesday | Renders the graphics | Films all 3 long videos and the Shorts in one session | — |
| Thursday–Friday | Writes descriptions, pinned comments and LinkedIn posts | Reviews the edits | Editors cut the long videos and Shorts |
| Next week | — | Publishes 3 long videos and 2 Shorts a day | Schedules the posts |
| Sunday | Builds the scorecard from YouTube Studio exports | 30-minute review: outliers, behind or on target | — |

### Who does what

- **Claude:** research, scripts, Shorts scripts, titles and thumbnail ideas, graphics, descriptions, LinkedIn posts, the weekly scorecard.
- **Chris:** locks titles, films, reviews edits, shows up in comments.
- **To hire:** 2 long-video editors, 1 Shorts editor, 1 thumbnail designer.
