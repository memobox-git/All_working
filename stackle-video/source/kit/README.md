# Kit

Shared pieces for Stackle's page-style videos (`../intro-pro`, `../asks`): fonts and palette (`kit.css`), the timeline and pen-stroke helpers (`kit.js`), Chris's photo (`img/`), pencil, paper and ambience sounds (`sfx/`), the voiceover cue writer (`make-cues.js`) and the soundtrack mixer (`mix.js`). Frames are rendered by `../render-pro.js`.
