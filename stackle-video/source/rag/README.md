## Rebuild

```bash

# preview, before the voice exists: a silent stand-in with estimated word timings
node ../explained/make-placeholder-vo.js . ../../scripts/rag-explained.md
./build.sh ../../graphics/rag-explained-preview.mp4 --sub 2
```

## The scenes

0. **Opening card.** The thumbnail.
1. **The problem, as the hook.**
   - "What's our refund policy?" types into a chat.
   - The policy file is there, but locked away from the model ("never in its training data").
   - The bot says it doesn't know, then makes up "60 days", and a "made up" stamp lands on it.
   - A bigger model gets crossed out.
   - The page flies into the model, and the answer becomes "30 days [1]" with its source.
   - "That's what RAG does.": the RAG title, then the channel intro.
2. **What it is.** R · A · G spell out into Retrieval · Augmented · Generation. Three steps light up in turn: Retrieve, Augment, Generate. Then the timeline: the 2020 paper (Facebook AI Research), then today.
3. **The core idea.**
   - A training bar fills up to its cutoff date, with your files outside it.
   - "Retrain the model" gets crossed out.
   - The prompt builds: the question, then the retrieved paragraph.
   - Open book vs closed book.
4. **Architecture.** Two lanes, with the camera following each part as it is named.
   - ① Indexing: documents → chunks → embeddings → the vector database's map of meaning, one colour per source.
   - ② Retrieval: question → embedding → a query point lands on the map, and its three nearest chunks light up → the chunks fly into the prompt → model → answer with sources.
   - The full view: the policy is updated, the answer goes out of date, only that file runs back through indexing, and the answer becomes 14 days.
5. **In short.** A job post with "RAG pipelines" highlighted, three takeaways, then "That's RAG." and the closing title.
6. **Comment ask.** "What should I explain next?"
