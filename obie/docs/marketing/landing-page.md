## 3. How it works (4 numbered steps, with the OBIE orb as mascot)
The orb's state changes in each step: listening, then idle, then working, then idle.

**01: Talk to OBIE.** *(orb: listening)*
Send a voice note or type. OBIE asks a few questions about what you sell, who buys it and what eats your time, then writes your Business Profile.

**02: Get your team.** *(orb: idle)*
OBIE recommends one agent for each part of your business: Sales, Marketing, Customers, Product, Operations, and custom ones like Community or Podcast. Switch on the ones you want with one tap.

**03: Give a goal.** *(orb: working)*
"Draft five LinkedIn posts by Friday." The agent plans it, shows the cost and splits the work into small tasks. Each task goes to a nano agent that does one job and disappears.

**04: Approve.** *(orb: idle)*
The drafts land in "Needs you". Approve, edit or reject each one. Nothing is sent or posted without your tap.

## 8. FAQ
**Is OBIE live?**
It's in private beta. Get early access and we'll invite you.

**Does it send emails or post for me?**
Not yet. Today OBIE drafts the work, and you copy it where it needs to go. One-click connections are next, and even then nothing goes out without your approval.

**Who is it for?**
Anyone running a business alone, or nearly alone, who does the same kinds of work across many areas: inquiries, follow-ups, posts, onboarding, updates.

**Can I use my voice?**
Yes. You can talk to OBIE from your phone, and you can edit the transcript before you send it.

**What are credits?**
Every task uses credits, and you see the estimate before anything runs. You start with 2,500 credits and get 100 more every day.

**How much will it cost?**
Pricing isn't set yet. Everyone who joins early gets a founding price before we launch publicly.

**What is OBIE Cloud?**
It's what we're building next: a customer database, a website and pages, forms and booking, and file storage, all in one place with one bill. It isn't live yet. Tell us which part you'd use first when you join.

**Who's building it?**
Chris, founder of Stackle. Stackle is OBIE's first customer and runs on it every day.
