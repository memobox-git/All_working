I've run 200+ data engineering interviews across banking, healthcare, automotive and financial exchanges.
What I don't care about, and what I do. 👇

What I don't care about:

Which cloud you used
→ AWS, Azure or GCP. Learn one well and the next one takes weeks.

Perfect syntax
→ Say "I'd look that up" and keep going. That's what engineers do every day.

A long list of tools
→ Spark, Airflow, dbt, Kafka on a resume shows what you touched, not what you understand.

What I do care about:

One pipeline, end to end
→ Where the data came from, what broke, and how you fixed it.

Questions before code
→ What does one row mean? How much data? Can it arrive late?

Trade-offs
→ Why batch and not streaming. Why that partition key. What it costs.

Checking your own numbers
→ Row counts before and after a join. NULLs. Duplicates.

Tools change every two years. How you think about data doesn't.
